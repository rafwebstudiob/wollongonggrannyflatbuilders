Building a granny flat in Berkeley

Berkeley is a southern suburb near Lake Illawarra, with post-war homes on suburban blocks.

Berkeley has many generous blocks, which makes it easier to meet lot-size and setback rules and still leave yard space for both households.

Post-war and 1980s homes often sit towards the front of the block, leaving a backyard that may suit a detached granny flat.
