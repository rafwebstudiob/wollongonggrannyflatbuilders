Do I need approval for a granny flat?

In NSW, a granny flat of up to 60 m² on a lot of at least 450 m² can often be approved as complying development if it meets all the Housing SEPP standards. Otherwise you lodge a DA with council. See our NSW granny flat approvals guide.

Can I rent out a granny flat?

Yes, granny flats in NSW can be rented out, but the lot can't be subdivided and there can only be one principal and one secondary dwelling. Talk to an accountant about tax before you rent it.
