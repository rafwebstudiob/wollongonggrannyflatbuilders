Granny flat costs in Wollongong: what affects the price

In short: We don't publish our own prices. A national cost guide puts most granny flats at about A$80,000–160,000, but a local builder has to quote your block.

Use published ranges only as a starting point, and check what each quote includes.

Last updated: 23 November 2026

We don't set or advertise our own prices. The figures below are from a published national cost guide, with a link. They are not quotes for Wollongong. A local builder has to price your block, design and inclusions.

Published cost ranges

  • hipages' granny flat cost guide (updated January 2026) says a granny flat typically costs about A$80,000–160,000, and high-spec custom builds can cost over A$200,000.
  • It puts a one-bedroom, 36 m² granny flat at about A$80,000–130,000 and a two-bedroom, 60 m² granny flat at about A$110,000–160,000.
  • The same hipages guide says NSW prices range widely, from about A$80,000 up to A$300,000, because of the number of builders and the range of specifications.

Advertised “from” prices often leave out site costs, connections and approvals, so compare what each quote includes.

What usually changes the quote

Size, layout and inclusions

Studio, one or two bedrooms; standard plan or custom design; and the finishes, kitchen and bathroom fit-out.

Site works

The city is squeezed between the Illawarra escarpment and the sea, so western suburbs are often steep, while the beachside strip is flatter. Slope, soil, trees, retaining, and access for machinery all affect the slab or footings and the earthworks.

Services

Sewer, stormwater, water and power connections, and any upgrades to the existing supply, are often big line items.

Approvals and certification

Certifier or council fees, plans, engineering and any extra reports (bush fire, flood or other overlays). See our NSW approvals guide.

Landscaping and outdoor areas

Decks, paths, fencing for privacy and outdoor space for each household.

About the budget field on our form

The budget bands are for you to show a range if you have one. They are not a price list.
